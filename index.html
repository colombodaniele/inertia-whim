Music

Inertia Whim are a London four-piece blending shimmery shoegaze, 90's alt noise, and a post-punk drive into one cohesive, distinct sound.

The soft spoken lyrics lie beside brooding, atmospheric melodies, which feels both ethereal and disorderly.
